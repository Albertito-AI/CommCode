## Foundations and future direction

CommCode aims to make software creation a capability people can own. Its current single-operator development workbench keeps projects in ordinary Git repositories and combines editing, tests, previews and reviewable model proposals. The revised paper explains current recovery/storage controls, their limits and the evidence still needed for live inference and user benefit.

Read the **[project whitepaper](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/CommCode/wiki/Whitepaper)** for definitions, worked examples and counterexamples, a claim-evidence map, reproduction instructions, related research and teaching questions.
