# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment,
discrimination, threats and disclosure of private information are not. Critique
claims and code with evidence. Make room for contributors with different languages,
abilities and levels of technical experience.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, pause discussions, or restrict
participation proportionately. Explain decisions and permit a respectful appeal
to an uninvolved maintainer where available. Security reports and personal disputes
belong in a private channel. A one-person project cannot promise an independent
appeal panel until such governance exists.
