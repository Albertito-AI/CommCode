# Governance and public-interest commitments

Albert Tan is the founding project steward. No independent foundation, elected
board or institutional endorsement is claimed. The source licence permits forks
and independent continuation. Contributors retain authorship and attribution.

Major technical decisions belong in public ADRs. Changes that affect licence,
privacy, institutional control or access need a public proposal and a documented
review period. As contributors join, establish additional maintainers and a clear
conflict-of-interest process. The roadmap records intentions, not commitments by
people who have not agreed to participate.

Future donations, grants, institutional support and optional support services may
fund maintenance. They may not justify intentionally crippling essential open-source
features. No sponsorship, donation account or commercial service is created by this
snapshot. Users retain the right to export projects and replace the platform.
